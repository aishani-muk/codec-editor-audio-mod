Saraga (Hindustani classical) — DSP back-end

Three raga clips (Yaman, Todi, Shree) under two $u(t)$ profiles. The DSP back-end is parametric EQ + compressor + pitch-shift, all conditioned on $u(t)$, and runs through the streaming pipeline at RTF $<$ 0.12. The trained Saraga LoRA-MusicGen editor (editor_v3_lora) is $u$-blind on this corpus and would produce bit-identical output across $u$, so it is represented in the metrics table on the project page rather than as inline audio.

Three observation patterns, one infrastructure

  • editor_v3_lora does not edit measurably. MD5s of the decoded output are bit-identical across $u\in\{0.0, 0.3, 0.6, 0.9\}$. Likely cause: T5 collapses the "intensity 0.x" suffix to near-identical embeddings.

  • editor_v3 calms aggressively in the metric direction. $\Delta A=-1.35$ at $u=0.6$, with substantial pitch-class and tonic drift; structural preservation is weak.

  • celtic_track_a sign-flips across domains. The Celtic-trained editor produces $\Delta A=-1.55$ on Celtic and $\Delta A=+0.47$ on Saraga; the same architecture trained on a different corpus moves the M2E score in opposite directions on the two test sets. The DSP baseline shows the same direction discrepancy on Hindustani vocal, suggesting the M2E arousal scorer (DEAM-trained on Western popular music) may carry a domain bias on Hindustani vocal.

  • $u$ is currently a switch, not a magnitude. Across the three trained editors, $u$-dependence ranges from absent (LoRA) to non-monotone (editor_v3) to single-step (celtic_track_a: $-1.55$ across all $u$). Smooth modulation has not yet been learned.
